Tools and prototypes from [The Usefulness of Art](https://www.tuoa.com.au).

## Tools

Packaged, tested and released.

- **[UsefulText](https://github.com/tuoa-tools/usefultext)** – extract text from images and PDF documents
- **[UsefulMedia](https://github.com/tuoa-tools/usefulmedia)** – an app to download media files
- **[UsefulRedact](https://github.com/tuoa-tools/usefulredact)** – checks whether the redaction in a document actually holds, entirely on your machine

## Prototypes

Experiments and research notebooks. Not packaged, and they may be rough.

- **[alt-text-create](https://github.com/tuoa-tools/alt-text-create)** – drafts alt-text for event images with a small open vision-language model, and measures how often it makes things up. Runs in Google Colab.
